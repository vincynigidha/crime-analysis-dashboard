General Safety Tips
  • Stay aware of your surroundings, especially at night or in unfamiliar areas.
  • Keep emergency numbers saved and easily accessible on your phone.
  • Report suspicious activity promptly, even if you're unsure it's significant.
  • Avoid sharing personal financial details over phone or email to unverified sources.
  • Inform a trusted person of your location when traveling alone at night.
